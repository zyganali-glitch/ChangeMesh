# Evidence and Autonomy Boundary

> In Antigravity Customizations, set this workspace rule to **Always On**.

- `BLOCKED` means underlying action remained `NOT_RUN`.
- `SIMULATED` cannot become real evidence.
- Model judgments cannot rewrite deterministic facts.
- Planned integrations remain `PLANNED` or `NOT_RUN`.
- Product behavior is autonomous by default.
- Do not add approval prompts where reversibility, policy, and rehearsal permit automatic work.
- Human escalation requires a compressed evidence packet.
