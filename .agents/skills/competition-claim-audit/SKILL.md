# Competition Claim Audit

For each claim identify exact artifact, command, trace, deployment revision, screenshot, or test; confirm it is current/sanitized; classify real, fixture, simulated, planned, or not run; remove unsupported language; synchronize duplicate claims; verify deadline/category/required technologies against official competition page.

Never improve a claim by hiding limitations.
